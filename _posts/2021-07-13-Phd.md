We offer Ph.D. positions in cooperation with NUS and NTU under the scholarships <a href="https://www.a-star.edu.sg/scholarships/home/scholarships/ags--scholarship">AGS</a> and <a href="https://www.a-star.edu.sg/scholarships/home/international-awards/astar-research-attachment-programme-arap">ARAP</a>. Please refer to the sites of the scholarships to see if you are eligible and don’t hesitate to reach out to us, if you are interested in a Ph.D. position in our lab. <br /><br />

<b>Possible research Topics:</b> <br />
- AI in genomics (cancer genome de novo assembly, genome and cell foundation models) <br />
- AI in structural biology (drug design, RNA therapeutics) <br /><br />

<b>AGS Ph.D. positions</b> (4 years period) <br />
<b>ARAP Ph.D. positions</b> (1 – 2 years period) – for ongoing international students <br /><br />
